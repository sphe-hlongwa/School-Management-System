Our Story

A heritage of excellence in Amanzimtoti since 1985

Mbambangwe High School was established with a vision to provide world-class education to the community of Amanzimtoti. Over the decades, we have evolved from a small local school into a premier institution of learning, known for our rigorous academics and outstanding sports programs.

Our mascot, the powerful Mbambangwe, represents the strength, focus, and resilience we instill in every student who passes through our gates.
