    Frequently Asked Questions

    What documents are required for application?

    You'll need: birth certificate, previous school reports, ID photos, proof of address, and immunization records.

    Is there an entrance examination?

    Yes, all applicants are required to take entrance exams in Mathematics and English. Dates are scheduled after application submission.

    What is the tuition fee structure?

    Tuition varies by grade. Please contact our finance office for detailed fee structure and payment plan options.
